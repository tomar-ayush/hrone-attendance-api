## Looked at and decided was fine

* **`load_dotenv()` at import time** – it does not override variables that are already set, so real environment variables win over `.env`, which is what the contract asks for.
* **Module-level `MongoClient`** – PyMongo's client is thread-safe and pooled; one client per process is the recommended pattern, and uvicorn's threadpool can share it.
* **`IST = timezone(timedelta(hours=5, minutes=30))`** – correct; IST has no DST, so a fixed offset is enough.
* **Sync (non-async) handlers** – FastAPI runs `def` endpoints in a threadpool, so blocking PyMongo calls do not block the event loop. Kept as is rather than switching to Motor.
* **Calendar strings stored as strings** – `date` / `joined_on` / shift times are compared lexicographically in queries, which is correct for zero-padded `YYYY-MM-DD` and `HH:MM`.
